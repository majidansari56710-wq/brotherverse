About

The story behind BrotherVerse

BrotherVerse started as a personal collection of photos and lines that never felt like enough — the kind of brotherhood that's hard to put into words, but easy to recognize the moment you feel it.

I'm Majid, the person behind BrotherVerse. This project began with twenty photos I couldn't bring myself to leave in a camera roll, and a habit of writing down the things I wished I'd said to my own brother sooner. BrotherVerse is that habit, turned into something you can use too.

Every quote here is written to sound the way brotherhood actually feels — messy, funny, protective, and quietly permanent. My hope is that you'll find a line that says what you've been meaning to say, pair it with a photo that means something, and send it to the brother who's earned it.
